01 · About

A bit about me

I am a passionate problem solver who is fascinated by the intersection of AI and Biotechnology. I love reading up about the fascinating ways in which nature adapts to various harsh circumstances. The world of synthetic biology is extremely fascinating since it opens up a whole new plethora of opportunities to solve day-to-day problems by taking inspiration from nature.

Professionally, I am a Data Scientist who has gained deep expertise in the fields of ML, DL, Gen AI and Agentic AI. Through my career at Deloitte, I have worked on Docuedge, a platform that turns messy, unstructured documents into structured data and searchable knowledge. My research experience during my undergraduate years has helped me work in the fields of Bioinformatics and Systems and Quantitative Biology.

Apart from work, I am a travel and adventure enthusiast, and I consider forests and wildlife my second home. I might be physically here, but my heart is lost in the forests of Kanchenjunga. Feel free to reach out to me if you plan to do an alpine trek someday!
